How it works

Your everyday moments become study material.

Abrody removes the distance between what you see and what you learn. The flow is simple: capture, understand, and review.

01Take a photo

Start from real scenes around you instead of a fixed textbook list.

02Find visual words

Objects in the photo become vocabulary cards with meaning and examples.

03Review quickly

Short sessions help you remember words through moments you actually saw.

Product flow

Photo first. Words second. Memory after that.

Instead of beginning with an abstract word list, Abrody begins with an image. You connect the word to something you actually saw, which makes the review experience more visual and personal.

Image-based vocabularyWords are attached to visible objects and scenes.
